## Business model (draft)
Revenue: marketplace commission (main), exhibition tickets, advertising on the website, advertising and sponsorship at exhibitions.
Costs: website and smart-contract development, deployment, hosting, staff, storage rent, security, insurance, exhibitions, marketing, taxes.

## Known issues in the model
1. Formulas use only the base scenario (column D); the other two are not connected.
2. Profit tax is calculated from revenue.
3. One-off development and deployment costs are spread over 12 months as operating costs.
4. The "Tickets" parameter is described as an amount but used as a quantity.
5. Key assumptions (10,000 deals a month with 20 paintings, average deal of 10 USD) are not justified.
