# Legal

Status: research not started. Nothing here is legal advice.

## Core question
How to make holding the NFT legally mean a right to a specific physical painting, and how that right is exercised: transfer, handing over the painting, disputes, inheritance.

## Questions to research
1. Legal qualification of the token in Russia (hypotheses to check: digital right under the Civil Code, digital financial asset, utility digital right) and what each implies for the operator and the buyers.
2. Who owns the painting: the foundation holding it for the token holder, or the token holder with the foundation as custodian.
3. The link between token and right: custody agreement, offer or issue terms, procedure for handing over the painting.
4. Payments: whether crypto payments are allowed in Russia and abroad; alternatives.
5. Foreign jurisdictions: choose 1–2 for comparison and check whether the token is a financial instrument there.
6. Taxes: foundation profit tax, buyers' and sellers' taxes, VAT on the commission.
7. Authors' rights: what passes with the token besides ownership of the original.
8. AML/KYC requirements.
9. Legal form of the foundation and of the DAO: who legally executes DAO decisions.

## Effect on other workstreams
If the token qualifies as a digital financial asset or a financial instrument, transfers may have to be restricted (whitelist) and a licensed operator may be required. This changes the contracts and the economics.

## Documents (target list)
Terms of use, marketplace rules, painting custody agreement, procedure for handing over a painting, privacy policy, AML/KYC policy, documents for the chosen foreign jurisdiction.
